Your phone, your PC, one sound system.

Echoport shares microphones and speakers between your devices over Wi-Fi or USB tethering. Use your phone as a wireless mic for Discord and Zoom, or listen to your PC through your phone's headphones.

Download free View the source

Free and open source under GPL-3.0 · No account · No time limits · Windows 10/11 and Android 8+

Two roles on every device

Send from one, play on another

A server sends its microphone or whatever it's playing. A player plays that into its speakers or into a virtual microphone other apps can use.

Phone mic →to PC virtual mic

A wireless microphone for Discord, Zoom, Teams or OBS.

PC audio →to phone speaker

Hear your computer on headphones plugged into your phone.

Phone mic →to PC speakers

A quick baby monitor or room-to-room intercom.

PC audio →to another PC's mic

Play music or sound effects straight into a call.

Features

Everything included, nothing locked

No time limit

Stay connected for as long as you like. No hourly disconnects, no subscription.

Mic boost up to 400%

Make a quiet microphone loud enough, with a soft limiter so it doesn't crackle.

Noise suppression

Removes keyboard clicks, fans and hum. Equalizer presets for clearer voice or more bass.

Finds devices on its own

Devices on the same Wi-Fi or USB tether show up automatically. Or scan a QR code.

Low, visible delay

A live chart shows the delay. Tune the buffer for less lag or fewer dropouts.

Private by design

Audio never leaves your network. Add a PIN so only your own devices can listen.

Many listeners

Several players can listen to one server at the same time. Record what you hear to WAV.

14 themes, two languages

Light and dark themes that follow your system. English and Persian with right-to-left layout.

How it works

Connected in three steps

  1. Install on both devices

    Windows installer or Android APK. For a virtual microphone on Windows, also install the free VB-Cable driver once.

  2. Start a server

    Pick the microphone or system audio and press Start server. Every network address of the device is listed, plus a QR code.

  3. Connect a player

    On the other device, pick the server from the list, scan the QR code or type its IP, and choose speakers or virtual mic.
